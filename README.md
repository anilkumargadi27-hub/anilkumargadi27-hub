# 💫 About Me:
I’m Gadi Anilkumar, a Computer Science undergraduate specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at Mohan Babu University, graduating in 2027. I’m interested in how thoughtful software engineering and AI can solve practical problems and make everyday work easier.<br><br>I enjoy the complete process of building a product—from understanding the problem and designing the user experience to developing the backend, integrating AI, and deploying a working application. My interests extend beyond getting a feature to work: I want to understand why it works, where it can fail, and how to make it more reliable.<br><br>Through independent projects and my Infosys Springboard internship, I’ve gained experience in full-stack development and AI integration. Building these applications has strengthened my ability to break down complex problems, troubleshoot unfamiliar issues, and take responsibility for a solution from idea to implementation.<br><br>Beyond development, coordinating Data Club activities and volunteering with NSS have helped me grow in communication, teamwork, and responsibility. I value consistent learning, constructive feedback, and explaining technical ideas in a way others can understand.<br><br>My goal is to grow into a software and AI engineer who combines strong technical fundamentals with an understanding of people’s needs—building useful products, contributing to a team, and continually improving through real engineering challenges.
